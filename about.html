About us

Your partners
in growth.

Based in West London, we provide innovative, growth-focused accounting solutions tailored to the needs of businesses and individuals.

Who we are

Built on relationships since 1985.

Manaktala & Co LTD began its journey in 1985.

From the very beginning, we have placed great value on the relationships we build with our clients. Those relationships remain a cornerstone of our firm.

Today, we are proud to still serve clients who have been with us for over 40 years, alongside many other long-term partners. We believe that enduring trust is a testament to the strength of our core values.

Regardless of how long we have worked together or the size of the business, we treat every client with the same respect and dedication.

What we do

Support that goes
beyond the numbers.

Comprehensive accounting services tailored to the unique needs of businesses and individuals across the UK.

We advise on business compliance, tax, payroll and risk, helping organisations remain informed, prepared and compliant.

For individuals, we support self-assessment filing, personal tax optimisation and the move to Making Tax Digital.

We build strong, long-lasting relationships and offer proactive support through challenges and opportunities at every stage of your business.

Our values

Passionate

We are deeply passionate about helping our clients reach their goals and aspirations. We go beyond simply providing services, actively seeking innovative and tailored solutions that align with each client’s specific needs. By collaborating across the firm and bringing together the strength and expertise of our team, we work to deliver the best possible results. We see ourselves as dedicated partners in our clients’ success, continually striving to unlock new opportunities and make a positive impact on their business and financial futures.

Personal

We believe every client is unique, with distinct needs, challenges and ambitions. Our approach begins by taking the time to understand our clients not only as businesses, but as individuals with their own values and objectives. This personal understanding allows us to offer advice and solutions aligned with their goals. Whether navigating complex financial decisions or planning for the future, we focus on what matters most and provide the guidance needed to achieve the desired outcome.

Professional

We hold ourselves to the highest professional standards, recognising that our reputation is built on the trust we earn. We invest continuously in our team’s technical expertise, interpersonal skills and knowledge of the latest accounting technology. This enables us to provide informed advice, build relationships founded on respect and trust, and deliver the consistently high-quality service our clients expect.
